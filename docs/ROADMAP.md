# ElysiaTools Roadmap

## V1.0 — First release

- Responsive Home, Conference Explorer, Journal Explorer, My Academic, Research Utilities.
- Curated CCF 2026 entries (not the full directory).
- Annual edition / track / event records, verified deadline display, AoE and IANA timezone conversion, monthly calendar and ICS.
- Journal comparison, verified metadata and explicit treatment of unknown fields.
- Local favorites, saved conference filters and JSON backup.
- DOI / arXiv / ISSN identifier links.
- Provenance policy, data tests and scheduled source-health reports.

## V1.1 — Full directory & visual intelligence

- CCF seventh edition: 386 conference entries plus 295 journal subject recommendation rows; four cross-listed titles deduplicated to 291 rated journal identities while maintaining all subjects.
- Source-backed multi-year acceptance rates for major conferences with explicit publication year, track, numerator/denominator where available.
- Interactive CCF A/B/C donuts, stacked field bars, conference acceptance charts and home dashboard with minimal external UI dependencies.
- Journal metrics shown as independent CAS 2025 historical, XinRui 2026 independent, JCR quartile, and metric-year JIF. Publisher-public indicators only; no copyright-violating redistribution.
- On-demand 2025 OpenAlex indexed publication counts with exact journal identity checks, publisher filters and richer journal comparison exports.

## Data coverage expansion

- Maintain full CCF grades and cross-disciplinary memberships; add independent official journal identifiers and official webpages where available.
- Add historic year/track timelines, verified deadline change records and permitted metrics.
- Develop conservative human-reviewed CFP change detection, distinguishing a broken page from an actual deadline change.

## V1.5 — My Academic

Multiple named watchlists, finer calendar filters, deadline change highlights, import previews and improved comparison export.

## V2.0 — Research Utilities

Add BibTeX cleaning, statistics, figure checks and CSV plots only after demonstrated demand. Avoid premature plugins, accounts, remote user storage or unverified AI recommendations.


## V1.2 — Conference history and local authorized rankings

- 273 CCF conference identities enriched with a total of 1,062 year/track statistical records; provenance distinguishes CCF-Deadlines community-transcribed figures from official, separately checked facts.
- Readable annual submission, accepted and rate tables with deep links and dynamic scale in the trend chart.
- Initial 8 individually source-linked public LetPub XinRui 2026 facts, kept distinct from official/authorized data.
- Fully offline CSV/TSV import workflow for authorized CAS 2025, XinRui 2026 and JCR2025 datasets; local storage, exact identifier matching, nonempty per-system filter counts and rich comparisons.
- Additional input parser tests and source integrity checks.


## V1.3 — Authorized reference synchronization (2026-10-12)

- Verified the upstream CAS 2025, XinRui 2026 and JCR 2025 CSV formats and expected overlap against 292 CCF-related journals.
- Added a user-directed one-click **local** sync path pinned to a stable ShowJCR revision, with explicit permission acknowledgment and manual-file fallback when GitHub Raw is unreachable.
- Corrected CAS bracketed zone values; preserved category-level CAS, XinRui and JCR classifications; marked synced third-party values separately from public and user-input metrics.
- Improved the open identity catalog with 246 print ISSNs and 250 electronic ISSNs through exact-title bibliographic cross-reference without republishing a restricted ranking database.
